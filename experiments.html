Experiments — Stackelberg Aircraft Patrol Planning

Experiments on the model exactly as implemented in the planner (no distances, no transit time between barriers). Every experiment calls the planner's own Stage 1, Stage 2, Stage 3, baseline and fleet-size functions from script.js, so the numbers here are the numbers the planner would produce. Randomised experiments are seeded, so a run is reproducible. Each one ends with a finding and a CSV you can put in the report.

Base configuration

Defaults follow the problem statement in the seminar report (§4.6): 22 aircraft, 4/3/3 barriers valued 3/2/1, 3 h sorties, safe for a further 6 h, aircraft unavailable for 3 h after a patrol, 55 h per aircraft over 30 days. Experiments sweep one or two of these and keep the rest fixed.
