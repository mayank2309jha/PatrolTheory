    Twenty ways to see one paper

    A table of numbers and a picture of the same numbers can teach completely different things. Each card below takes one piece of the paper and draws it in the form that suits it best: flows as a Sankey, trade-offs as curves, probabilities as dots you can count, schedules as Gantt bars and clocks, the game itself as a tree, and the randomness as a live simulation.

    How to read each card Shows: which part of the paper. Why this form: what the picture makes obvious that the table hides. Unless a card says otherwise, the numbers are the paper's own 22-aircraft example (n = 4, 3, 3; v = 30, 20, 10; τp = 3 h; τs = 6 h; T = 30 days; F = 55 h).
